Creating A Protagonist

◆

Each Protagonist is defined by a Race, a Class, four Attributes, a set of Skills, a Trait, a Burden, and their Hit Points.

The player has 10 points to distribute among Hit Points, Attributes, and Skills. The Class determines costs, limits, and options.

Attribute Costs

LevelPrimary Attr.Non-Primary
Untrained → Competent1 point2 points
Competent → Expert2 points4 points
Expert → Exceptional4 points8 points

Each Hit Point costs 1 point. Each Skill or Grimoire costs 1 point.

The racial bonus grants a 1-point discount on purchasing Competent in the racial family's attribute (free if it matches the class's primary attribute). The discount applies only to that step.

Trait

A phrase describing the character's core nature. No mechanical effect — narrative fuel for the Narrator and a guide for the player.

"I leave no one behind" · "Power is taken, not asked for" · "I prefer an uncomfortable truth to a kind lie"

Burden

A weight the character carries: a regret, a debt, a secret, a curse. The Narrator uses it to create dilemmas.

"I killed an innocent during the war" · "I owe my life to someone I hate" · "Someone wears my face"
