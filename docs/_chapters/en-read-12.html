Exertion

◆

"I bleed to make it happen."

Before a roll, the player may declare Exertion: add +1d6 to the roll. After the roll, take 1 Fatigue Wound. This extra die counts for both the result and the Critical.

Exertion is limited to +1d6 per action — it does not stack. Since the Wound is applied after the roll, state penalties (Battered, Severe) do not affect the current action but future ones.

A Mystic with 3 HP has spent 1 on a Grimoire. 2 HP left. Needs TN 5. Declares Exertion: adds 1d6 and rolls at full power. After the roll, pays 1F and drops to 1 HP (Severe). If they fail and take a hit, they're out. At the last throw.

Exertion outside combat

The Scholar must translate an ancient inscription before the temple collapses. Lore Expert, TN 5. Rolls 2d6↑: 3 and 4. Failure.

Declares Exertion: 1F, adds 1d6. Now three dice. Gets 2, 4, 6. Success! But down to 2 HP — battered. When the floor gives way, he'll have to run on trembling legs.
