Exertion

◆

"I bleed to make it happen."

Before a roll, the player may declare Exertion: add +1d6 to the roll. After the roll, take 1 Fatigue Wound. This extra die counts for both the result and the Critical.

Exertion is limited to +1d6 per action — it does not stack. Since the Wound is applied after the roll, state penalties (Battered, Severe) do not affect the current action but future ones.

A Mystic with 3 HP has spent 1 on a Grimoire. At 2 HP they are Battered (-1 tier). With Wonder Expert, that means 1d6. They need TN 5 and declare Exertion before rolling: +1d6 makes 2d6, keeping the highest. After the roll, they pay 1F and fall to 1 HP (Severe). If they fail and take a hit, they are out of the scene. At the last throw.

Exertion outside combat

The Scholar must translate an ancient inscription before the temple collapses. Lore Expert, TN 5. He declares Exertion before rolling: 3d6, keep the highest. Gets 2, 4, 6. Success! After the roll he pays 1F, falling to 2 HP — battered. When the floor gives way, he must run on trembling legs.
