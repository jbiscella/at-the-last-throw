There are three people in Hollow Hearth who matter to your investigation. The Protagonists may speak with all three, in any order. Each holds one piece of the picture, and at least one of them is almost lying.

Refer to map-hollow-hearth-village.svg for the layout. All village locations are within Near distance of one another.

Hesta Thrush — The Mayor

Hesta Thrush (NPC) — Lore Competent. No combat stats; if violence touches her, she is what she appears to be.

A woman in her sixties, weather-cracked, narrow with grief. Her son left for the war and never came home; she has been mayor for eleven years and knows everyone in the village by middle name.

What she knows freely:

  • The brigands came from the Old Watchtower, four hours' walk north into the Whispering Wood.
  • She offers, as the village's final reward, a relic from the founding: the Blade of Salt (manual p. 11).

What she will lie about:

She knows her cousin Marrel was seen drinking with one of the brigands at the inn the week before the theft. She does not want her cousin hanged for it. A Lore roll, TN 4 (opposed by Hesta's Lore Competent) catches the discomfort. Diplomacy convinces her to come clean; Rhetoric likewise. Intimidation, TN 5 also works, but turns the village against the party for the rest of the adventure.

Trait/Burden hook

A Protagonist whose Burden involves war, lost children, or broken oaths will find Hesta either an ally or a mirror. Lean into it. "You remind me of him," she says, looking at the wrong person.

Old Bram — The Bellringer

Old Bram (NPC) — Lore Competent. Cannot fight any longer.

The bellringer, in his eighties, who survived because he was relieving himself in the brambles when the brigands came. The boy who died was his apprentice and his great-nephew. He is angry. Not loud. The other kind.

What he saw:

  • Four men. One in armour, one with a longbow, two with daggers.
  • The armoured one had a white sun stitched on the shoulder of his cloak — the heraldry of a knightly order disgraced ten years ago. A Lore roll, TN 4 with the History skill identifies the order as the Order of the Pale Sun, dissolved for "ritual offences" in the wars. Without History, TN 5 to dredge up vague memory.
  • He believes the bell is alive. He may be right.

Vela Roan — The Herbalist

Vela Roan (NPC) — Lore Expert (Herbalism, Survival), Wonder Competent. Will not fight unless cornered.

Forty, sharp, the only person in the village who has been into the Whispering Wood and come back. She knows what the Hollow Folk are. She will not say it plainly. She speaks in stories.

What she offers, if asked the right way:

  • The "bark-people" come from the roots when no bell rings. She believes one Hollow person is already walking among the villagers in mortal guise.
  • She will give a vial of distilled nightroot — a notable object: poured on a blade, the next blow inflicts +1 Wound against unnatural creatures; one use only — if a Protagonist agrees to bring her a sprig of moonsilver flower from deep in the wood.

This last is a side temptation: a chance for a Burden moment if anyone breaks their word later, or a chance for an honourable Trait to be tested. The flower exists. Whether the party finds time for it is up to them.

Optional · Marrel the Cousin

If the Protagonists pursue Hesta's lie, Marrel can be found at the inn, drinking through his fear. He confirms the brigand he spoke with — a man named Thessik — bragged about a buyer who "pays in northern silver" coming for the bell. Marrel did not know it would be stolen. Intimidation, TN 4 breaks him. Diplomacy, TN 4 with a drink also works. Commerce, TN 4 if a Scholar wants to talk through what "northern silver" actually means (it is a term used by smugglers crossing the border — northern silver does not exist, it is a code for fey gold).

Teaching moment for the Narrator

When a player declares "I want to know if she's lying," ask them how. Are they pressing? Watching? Catching her in a contradiction? The how determines the Attribute and skill. Lore is the default for reading a person; Etiquette catches her in courtly slips, Intimidation forces her into mistakes, Deception lets you out-lie her into a corner. There is no separate social-combat subsystem. There is just Lore plus a relevant skill against a TN. Same as everything else.
