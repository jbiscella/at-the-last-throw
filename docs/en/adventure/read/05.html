Resolution

Three clean endings, with consequences that seed the next adventure:

The Bell Returns. Hesta awards the Blade of Salt and the villages full hospitality. Velas vial as well, if her flower was brought back. Word spreads to the next town. The next adventure begins with the party being sought out by name.

The Bell is Silenced or Sold. The party leaves rich and marked. The Hollow Folk note them. A future adventure begins with whispers in the night that know their names.

The Bell is Destroyed. Whether by an unlucky Backlash or a desperate fight, the Hollow Folk return to Hollow Hearth on the moonless night. The next adventure begins with the party trying to undo it. Veyl Ashen, the original Mystic, may not be as dead as the histories say.

The Three Questions

End the session by having the group answer the three Advancement questions together (manual p. 15). An honest accounting — not a generous one — is the right one. A new player who blocked at every turn earned no advancement points. A new player who tried, failed, and reckoned with consequence earned three. Make this clear, gently, the first time.
