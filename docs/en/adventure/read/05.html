Following the manual's enemy format (p. 12): TN (Strength/Dexterity) · Level · Wounds · Weapon · Behaviour.

Sir Corwin Vask, Disgraced Knight TN S4/D5 · Expert · 6 Wounds · longsword (2W Str) · heavy armour (included in his TN). Behaviour: "Will not strike a man on his knees. Will duel anyone who calls him 'sir'. Hates Mystics — his order taught him to." Optional: he carries a battered locket with a portrait of his sister inside. If a Protagonist sees it during the fight, give them a chance to use it.

Brigand TN S3/D3 · Competent · 2 Wounds · dagger (2W Dex) or short club (1F Str). Behaviour: "Cowards in a fair fight. Flee if Corwin falls." As a group: roll once, +1W per member beyond the first (max +3). Each Wound dealt to the group eliminates one member.

The Buyer (Hollow Person in mortal guise) TN S3/D4 · Expert · 4 Wounds · touch (1F Wonder; on Critical, 2W + a Condition that lasts the rest of the scene) · no armour. Behaviour: "Speaks the wrong syllables. The trees do not answer. Bleeds sap, not blood."

Starved Wolf (optional Wood encounter) TN S3/D4 · Competent · 2 Wounds · bite (2W Str). Behaviour: "Hunts the weakest. Three nights without food."

Hesta Thrush (NPC) — Lore Competent. No combat stats; if violence touches her, she is what she appears to be.

Vela Roan (NPC) — Lore Expert (Herbalism, Survival), Wonder Competent. Will not fight unless cornered.

Old Bram (NPC) — Lore Competent. Cannot fight any longer.
