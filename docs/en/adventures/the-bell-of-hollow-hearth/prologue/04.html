For the Narrator only. This chapter reveals the secrets of “The Bell of Hollow Hearth”. If you will play the adventure as a player, stop here.

This prologue leads into “The Bell of Hollow Hearth”. If your players have read it, or if you played it, Nim and Mara arrive in the village with a letter and a false coin. The adventure does not mention them: here is how to use them.

The letter. The buyer of the bell wrote it, and it was meant for Thessik, the brigand who drank with Marrel at the inn. “Half the debt” is the price already paid to the brigands; the other half will come with the buyer, to the tower, at midnight.

Hesta. She knows nothing about the letter, but she recognises the inn and understands that it leads to her cousin Marrel. She turns pale and says the inn serves anyone. The opposed Lore roll in Scene 1 uncovers her lie as usual.

Marrel. If he is shown the letter, he recognises the words “northern silver”: they are the ones Thessik used.

At the tower. The buyer says the right words with the stress in the wrong places, and his purse of silver is closed with a two-headed wax seal, the same as the false coin. Anyone who followed the prologue can recognise him as the man who paid for the theft. The recognition does not reveal that he is one of the Hollow Folk: the players only know what that silver buys, and the choice of the twist remains theirs.

The two toll-men. They are brigands from Corwin’s band, sent to keep the road clear and to get the letter back. If you like, the man with the scar can be one of the brigands at the tower.
